This project implements a 1-bit Full Adder in Verilog HDL.

### Inputs
- A
- B
- Cin (Carry Input)

### Outputs
- Sum
- Cout (Carry Output)

## Logic Equations
Sum = A ^ B ^ Cin

Cout = (A & B) | (B & Cin) | (A & Cin)A B Cin | Sum Cout
